One sentence, at most 200 characters, explaining the modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800: a post office and library as wide as the world, where a letter crosses the sea in a blink and a thousand books lie open on your desk. Built only from words and pictures a person from 1800 could already know.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (set `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` to write elsewhere).
